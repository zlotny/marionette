Download the APK Free · v1.3 · 44 MB · Android 10+
Why an APK and not the Play Store?

To act for you, Marionette needs access that Google Play limits: sending text messages, seeing every app you have installed, operating other apps through accessibility, and using your location in the background. A store version would lose those abilities. The APK keeps all of them, and you still choose which ones to switch on.

Marionette is the hands. Your agent is the brain.

Bring the agent you like. Marionette gives it your voice and your phone. Your agent does the thinking, and it can keep learning: new skills, your habits, your preferences.

Not a fixed assistant. One that grows with you.

Most assistants ship as they are and stay that way. Marionette starts simple and improves with each small correction you make.

It learns your voice

  • Train your wake phrase with your own voice, close up and across the room.
  • A false wake? Mark it with one tap so it stops happening.
  • Read a short text in your normal voice. It learns what is not the wake phrase.
  • Review what woke it, and keep it sharp.

Your agent learns too

  • Bring the agent you like, and change it whenever you want.
  • A self-hosted agent learns new skills and remembers your preferences, on your own server.
  • Pick the model and how hard it thinks, per chat.
  • With a plain model, edit its instructions in plain words.

Open. Private. Yours.

The wake phrase runs on your phone. Marionette talks directly to the agent you choose, with no middleman and no subscription. Your keys stay encrypted on your phone.

Every tool, permission and confirmation is a switch. Screen control stays off until you turn it on.
