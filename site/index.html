Marionette is a voice assistant for Android that really does things. Say your phrase and talk. It runs your phone, and hands everything else to your own AI agent.

Download the APK Version 1.0. Free and open source. Android 10 or newer.
Why an APK and not the Play Store?

To act for you, Marionette needs access that Google Play limits: sending text messages, seeing every app you have installed, operating other apps through accessibility, and using your location in the background. A store version would lose those abilities. The APK keeps all of them, and you still choose which ones to switch on.

Your voice, your phone, your agent.

Most assistants stop at the edge of the phone. Marionette has two hands. The phone does what a phone can do. Your agent does the rest, on your accounts, your home and your servers.

Only what you allow. Only what you ask for.

Everything is a switch you control. Give Marionette as little or as much as you like, and change your mind at any time.

What the phone exposes

  • Turn off any tool. If calls are off, it can't call.
  • Grant each permission on its own: contacts, calendar, notifications, location.
  • Choose what needs a spoken yes before it happens.
  • Screen control stays off until you turn it on, and you can block apps from it.
  • Choose what context it shares, like location or what's playing.

What your agent does

  • Your agent runs where you put it, with the tools you give it.
  • Connect your email, your home, your servers, or none of them.
  • Pick the model and how hard it thinks, per chat.
  • With a plain AI model, edit its instructions in plain words.
